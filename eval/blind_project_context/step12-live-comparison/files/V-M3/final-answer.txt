If the **absolute clock offset exceeds two seconds**, the gateway must pause schedule execution, notify the duty operator, and leave queued reservations unchanged. It may resume only after **two consecutive checks against the observatory reference** each report an offset within two seconds. [clock.md:3–7](/tmp/opencode/step12-live-work/project/clock.md#L3-L7)
