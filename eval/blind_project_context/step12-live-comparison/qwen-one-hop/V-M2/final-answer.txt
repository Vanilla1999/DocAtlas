- **Stop outdoor observations** when the wind interlock opens. Preserve reservation identifiers during the suspension. [weather.md, lines 5–8](weather.md#L5-L8)
- **Restart only** after the interlock closes **and** the duty operator records restart approval. A favourable forecast cannot authorize a restart. [weather.md, lines 5–8](weather.md#L5-L8); [lines 13–15](weather.md#L13-L15)
- **An indoor instrument may continue** during a wind suspension if it is explicitly classified as indoor and its own safety interlocks remain closed. A roof shown as closed on the calendar does not establish indoor classification. [weather.md, lines 9–12](weather.md#L9-L12)
