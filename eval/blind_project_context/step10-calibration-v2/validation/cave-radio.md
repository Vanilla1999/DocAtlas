# Cave team equipment guide

This guide contains separate equipment procedures and storeroom information for the cave survey team. A heading's instructions apply only to the equipment it names.

## Underground radio check

This procedure applies to the low-frequency radios used by the survey team below ground. Surface vehicle radios use a different check sheet.

### Antenna connection

Connect the loop antenna to the marked socket before enabling the transmitter. Lay the loop flat and keep its cable clear of the footpath.

### Voice exchange

Ask the surface operator to repeat the test phrase exactly and record the reply in the trip log. A carrier indication alone does not count as a completed radio check.

### Standby

After a successful voice exchange, place the transmitter in receive-only standby until the scheduled check-in. Leave the antenna connected during standby.

### Storage labels

The radio cases have orange inventory labels. Replacement labels are available in the stationery tray; their colour does not indicate battery charge or signal quality.

## Flood watch exception

During an active flood watch, the underground radio check does not authorize entering the cave even if the test phrase is repeated correctly. The expedition leader must formally clear the flood watch before entry.

## Membership forms

New team members submit membership forms to the secretary. Blank forms are printed on recycled paper and kept beside the visitor book.
