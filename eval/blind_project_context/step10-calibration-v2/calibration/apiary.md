# Cooperative apiary handbook

This handbook collects independent work instructions and building information for the cooperative apiary. Each named procedure has its own scope.

## Moving occupied hives

The instructions in this section apply only to occupied hives moved between stands inside the same apiary. Moving a hive to another site requires a separate transport plan.

### Entrance preparation

Close the hive entrance with a ventilated transport screen after the last flying bees have returned. Keep the screen unobstructed throughout the move.

### Securing the boxes

Fasten the hive boxes and floor together with two ratchet straps before lifting. Lift the secured hive with two people and place it level on the new stand.

### Reopening

Remove the transport screen after the hive is stable on its new stand. Place a leafy branch across the entrance to encourage returning bees to reorient.

### Trolley paint

The equipment trolley is painted cream. Touch-up paint and brushes are kept in the garden shed; paint colour does not identify a hive's destination.

## Queen inspection exception

An occupied hive awaiting completion of a queen inspection must not be moved between stands, even when the entrance has been screened and the boxes are strapped. Complete and record the queen inspection before scheduling the move.

## Visitor badges

Visitors collect yellow paper badges from reception and return them before leaving. Badges do not record hive health or inspection status.
