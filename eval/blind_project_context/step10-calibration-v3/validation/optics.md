# Meridian mirror workshop

This handbook covers the workshop's removable observatory mirrors. It does not prescribe work on permanently mounted lenses.

## Packing a mirror

Use this procedure only for a cooled, removable mirror. The [crack restriction](#crack-restriction) applies even after packing is complete.

### Cooling

Leave the mirror on its padded bench for forty minutes before wrapping it. Do not start wrapping while the surface remains warmer than the room.

### Wrapping

Place acid-free tissue against the reflective face, followed by a rigid cover. Never put adhesive tape directly on the reflective coating.

### Crate closure

Fasten both brass latches and have a second technician record the crate number before moving it to dispatch.

### Bench accessories

The bench pencil cup is violet. Replacement pencils are stored in drawer six; their colour is not an inspection result.

## Crack restriction

A mirror with a visible edge crack must remain in the inspection room, even if both crate latches are fastened. Obtain the optics supervisor's written release before moving that mirror to dispatch.
