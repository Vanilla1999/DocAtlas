# Reservoir station

These procedures apply to the unmanned reservoir station during winter operation.

## Restart

Restart the circulation pump only after the inlet sensor reads at least four degrees Celsius. Record the sensor reading in the shift ledger before pressing START.

### Inspection

Before restart, inspect the intake grille and remove accumulated leaves. A cracked grille requires replacement before the pump may run.

### Indicator lamps

The amber cabinet lamp indicates that the maintenance drawer is open. Closing the drawer extinguishes this lamp; the lamp does not measure inlet temperature.

## Frozen intake

When ice remains visible inside the intake, restarting the circulation pump is prohibited even if the inlet sensor reads four degrees Celsius or more. Request a manual thaw inspection instead.

## Ledger storage

Completed shift ledgers are archived in the west cabinet for nine months. The cabinet key is held by the records clerk.

## Cabinet finish

The exterior cabinet is painted slate blue. Replacement paint tins are stored with the building supplies.
