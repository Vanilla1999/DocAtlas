# Observatory archive

These instructions govern the observatory's physical photographic plates, including plates borrowed from partner observatories.

## Dispatch

Dispatch a photographic plate only after its catalogue number has been entered on a padded crate label. The receiving curator must acknowledge the inventory before the crate leaves the archive.

### Packing

Place each plate in an acid-free sleeve with the emulsion facing away from the seam. Separate sleeved plates with rigid spacers.

### Label printer

The label printer uses violet ribbon. Its paper tray is refilled from the lower stationery drawer; printer ribbon colour does not identify plate ownership.

## Partner restriction

A photographic plate borrowed from a partner observatory must not be dispatched until the lending institution gives written permission, even when the receiving curator has acknowledged the inventory.

## Visitor register

Archive visitors sign a paper register retained for fourteen months. The register is kept at the reception desk.

## Corridor lighting

The archive corridor lights switch off at twenty-one hundred hours. A separate emergency light remains illuminated overnight.
