- **Stop outdoor observations when the wind interlock opens.** Preserve reservation identifiers during the suspension.
- **Restart only after both** the interlock closes and the duty operator records restart approval. A favourable forecast is not an interlock signal and cannot authorize a restart.
- **An indoor instrument may continue** during the wind suspension if it is explicitly classified as indoor and its own safety interlocks remain closed. A roof shown as closed on the calendar does not establish that classification.
