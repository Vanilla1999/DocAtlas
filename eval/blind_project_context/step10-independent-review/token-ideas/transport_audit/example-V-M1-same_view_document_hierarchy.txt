## Transfer

Transfer a reservation only after the receiving observer accepts it and the duty operator approves the transfer. Retain the original reservation identifier and record both observers in the audit trail. A transfer does not alter the [weather suspension](weather.md#suspension).


```

## Locked instrument

If the instrument has an active maintenance lock, leave the transfer pending even after observer acceptance and operator approval. The lock must be cleared by the instrument team first.


```
