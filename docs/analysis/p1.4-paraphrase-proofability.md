# P1.4 — Paraphrase and proofability robustness

Status: **closed with provider-free measured evidence**.

This protocol separates two questions that were previously easy to conflate:

1. did the query plan expose enough concepts to discover the candidate source;
2. did the final production evidence selector prove every mandatory obligation
   strongly enough to return `supported`?

A candidate hit is therefore never counted as documentary support by itself.

## Frozen corpus and result

Fourteen cases contain exactly two rows for each family:

```text
exact_identifier
behavior
requirements
policy
typo
alias
negative_control
```

Measured result:

```text
required discovery:                  10/10
required final support:               5/5
negative-control false support:       0/2
all candidate discoveries:           10/14
all supported answers:                7/14
```

The five hard support cases are the two exact-identifier rows, the two core
behavior rows, and the exact requirements row. Broader requirements and policy
variants are recorded independently; candidate discovery may succeed while the
selector still returns `insufficient_evidence` because mandatory proof could not
be localized.

Typos and natural-language aliases are measurements, not authority to widen
retrieval or weaken support. In this corpus aliases were discoverable but did
not become hard support claims; typo rows remained undiscovered. Those outcomes
remain visible instead of being averaged into the core acceptance result.

## Safety boundary

Every negative control remained unsupported. The report also rejects:

- support without selected evidence;
- a required discovery miss;
- a required core support miss;
- an inconsistent support status;
- an absolute local path;
- a claim that the provider-free protocol changed production runtime, public
  MCP schemas, support semantics, or proved autonomous Agent Truth.

## Decision

The exact/core proofability decision is `accepted`, and negative-control
precision is `accepted`.

No production retrieval, public MCP schema, authority rule or support rule is
changed by this slice. Product maturity remains **Beta**. The next evidence
question is P1.5: whether mixed source roles preserve claim-local provenance
under conflict.
