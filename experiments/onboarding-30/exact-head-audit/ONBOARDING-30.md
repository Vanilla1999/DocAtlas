# Thirty newcomer questions: actual answer review

Runtime `bf663cb9838fe326579f258cffd05424fa003fbb`, 2026-09-06, PR #178.

All 30 fixed questions were called without initial lookups. After the public first outputs, 24 proposals and six declines were sealed before retry execution. The same inputs were replayed after the repair. Each accepted retry replaced the baseline whole; no sources from separate payloads were combined.

These are manual, exposed coordinator judgments about the returned evidence, not independent-model or holdout scores. `ok` is not sufficiency. Counts: **12 sufficient, 10 partial, 5 off-topic, 2 no evidence, 1 unestablished capability**. The encryption control does not justify claiming that a feature is absent.
